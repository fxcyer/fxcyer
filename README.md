## 🏛️ Featured Engineering Work

### 1 · YAKAP Patient Management System

<table width="100%">
  <tr>
    <td>
      <p><strong>Lead Developer @ Municipal Health Office (MHO Pinamalayan)</strong></p>
      <p>A web-based patient management platform now in active use by municipal healthcare staff — digitizing patient records, PhilHealth verification tracking, vaccine/vial inventory, and internal staff communications.</p>
      <table width="100%">
        <tr><th align="left">Problem</th><th align="left">Solution</th></tr>
        <tr><td>Paper-based patient records with no audit trail</td><td>Full digitization with Firestore-backed records and structured role-based access control</td></tr>
        <tr><td>No visibility into vaccine/vial stock levels</td><td>Custom vial utilization & dose-tracking module built from scratch, with a mock offline-first layer for pre-deployment testing</td></tr>
        <tr><td>Manual, disorganized update announcements</td><td>SaaS-style changelog system with timeline layout, type filters, search, and an announcement publisher</td></tr>
        <tr><td>Inconsistent access across staff roles</td><td>Three-tier RBAC (SuperAdmin, Staff/Encoder, Viewer) enforced at both UI and data layer</td></tr>
        <tr><td>Legacy UI slowing the team down</td><td>Ongoing migration from inline styles/Bootstrap to Tailwind CSS, with memoized components and debounced search for performance</td></tr>
      </table>
      <p><strong>Key modules:</strong> Patient records · PhilHealth verification · Vial utilization & dose tracking · Settings & changelog system · Calendar view · Super admin publisher</p>
      <p>
        <code>React (Vite)</code> <code>Firebase / Firestore</code> <code>FastAPI</code> <code>Tailwind CSS</code>
      </p>
    </td>
  </tr>
</table>

## ❓ How I Work

<details>
<summary><b>What kind of projects do you take on?</b></summary>
<br>
Primarily systems for institutions — health offices, government units, and organizations that need reliable record-keeping, role-based access, and reporting. I also take on data encoding and analytics work outside of that scope.
</details>
<br>

<details>
<summary><b>Do you work solo or with a team?</b></summary>
<br>
Both. As Lead Developer on YAKAP PMS I own architecture decisions end-to-end, but I'm comfortable working within an existing team's codebase and conventions.
</details>
<br>

<details>
<summary><b>What does your development process look like?</b></summary>
<br>
Start with the real workflow the system needs to replace (usually paper-based), map that to a data model, then build role-based access around it before touching UI polish. Full-file rewrites over patchwork when a component needs real restructuring.
</details>
<br>

<details>
<summary><b>Are you available for freelance work?</b></summary>
<br>
Yes — open to select freelance contracts alongside full-time opportunities. Best fit: civic tech, health tech, internal admin tools, and data analytics dashboards.
</details>
<br>

<details>
<summary><b>What's your strongest technical area?</b></summary>
<br>
Full-stack web systems with React/Vite on the frontend and FastAPI/Firebase on the backend, plus structured RBAC design — the layer most systems get wrong.
</details>
